# Stakeholder Interview

## 1. Introduction
We conducted a survey to understand how UMT students manage their personal finances. The information collected will be used to identify the problems and needs of students.

## 2. Stakeholders
The main stakeholders for this project are UMT students because they are the target users of the MyWallet system.

## 3. Data Collection Method
We used Google Forms to collect information from students. The survey was shared online and students answered the questions based on their own experiences.

## 4. Respondents
A total of 23 responses were collected. Out of these, 17 respondents were UMT students.

## 5. Interview Questions
The survey included questions about:
- How students manage their monthly money.
- How they record their daily expenses.
- Problems they face when managing money.
- Whether they set a monthly budget.
- Features they want in a financial management system.

## 6. Findings
The survey responses showed that students face some difficulties in managing their personal finances. Some students do not record their expenses regularly and may have problems controlling their spending. 
The findings will be used to identify the requirements for the MyWallet system.
